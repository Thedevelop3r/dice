  Luck Bet (src/games/luckbet.rs) — 8 dice named A–H, max 3 players (you + up to
  2 CPUs, difficulty selectable). You back a letter and the face it'll land on,
  defaulting to 6 but any 1–6 is allowed. A hit pays 5:1 from the house (6:1
  with a VIP pass); a miss loses the stake and rakes 20% into a carried-over 
  jackpot. If your number lands and three or more dice show it, you sweep the 
  jackpot. The winning die lights up green after settling. Verified live: CPU-2
  hit E-on-6 with 3 sixes on the table → +80 chips  HIT + SWEEP (3 copies).

  Luck Bet: Turbo — same game, no betting input. Enter spins the table at
  exactly 20 fps for 3 seconds (60 frames, redrawn in place with cursor-rewind
  and a hidden cursor), settles, then press Enter to continue. Bets repeat from
  your last one automatically. Confirmed in tmux: still spinning at 1.9s,
  settled by ~3.1s.

  Store (src/shop.rs + src/economy.rs) — chips ↔ dollars both ways (10 chips per
  $1 buying, 12 per $1 cashing out — the spread is the house edge), plus goods:
  Reroll Token, Insurance Chit, Lucky Charm, VIP Table Pass (6:1 payouts), Gold
  Dice skin. Verified: bought $5→50 chips, insurance, charm, cashed out 24
  chips→$2; balances reconciled exactly. Both consumables fired correctly in a
  live round — the charm rerolled the missed die, insurance refunded half the
  stake, and both decremented to 0.

  Tournament (src/games/tournament.rs) — chip buy-in, three tiers (10/4-player,
  25/8-player, 100/8-player Hard field), shuffled draw, single-elimination Pig.
  Your matches are played interactively; the other branches simulate silently
  via a new pig::simulate. Champion takes 70% of the pool, runner-up 30%.

  Related things I added: one shared wallet across Luck Bet, Chuck-a-Luck and
  tournaments (Chuck-a-Luck's separate bankroll is migrated in, not lost); a
  broke-player bailout so no mode dead-ends; stats and rules sections for every
  new mode; and inventory in the stats screen.

  One bug worth flagging: your prompt_usize retry loop spun forever on EOF,
  which hung my first piped test for two minutes. Prompts now latch an EOF flag
  and fall back to defaults, so the app stays scriptable.
